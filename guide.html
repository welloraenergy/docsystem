DocSystem Quick Guide

A short guide for everyday document work. Available tools depend on your access and the selected document template.

Global SettingsTemplatesShortcodesButtons & Documents

1. Global Settings

Company details, logo, addresses, contact information, header, footer and other common settings are maintained centrally. Where available, these values are reused in documents to keep company presentation consistent.

If a shared detail needs changing, check the relevant settings rather than manually changing it in every new document.

2. Preset & Custom Templates

Preset Templates provide ready-made layouts. Select the document family, enter the required details and review the result.

Custom Templates let you work with a configured layout for other document needs. Choose the appropriate template source in Workspace before entering information.

3. Shortcodes

Shortcodes are reusable placeholders in headers, footers and template text. They display the related information automatically when the document is prepared.

Examples: {logo} for the logo, {companyname} for the company name, {doctype} for document type and {page} for page numbering. Available shortcodes depend on the template settings.
