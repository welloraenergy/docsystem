01 · Global Settings

Shared company details

Common information can be reused across documents to maintain a consistent appearance.

Business information

Company name, logo, registration details, address and contact information are maintained in shared settings by authorized users.

Document appearance

Header, footer and relevant template settings control how company information appears in documents.

02 · Templates

Preset Templates

Choose the document family and type in Workspace before entering document details.

Preset Templates

Ready-made document layouts for common company paperwork. Select the document family and complete the relevant fields.

03 · Shortcodes

Reusable placeholders

Shortcodes insert information into supported header, footer or template areas.

Examples: {logo} displays the company logo, {companyname} the company name, {doctype} the document type, and {page} the page number.

Use the shortcodes provided by the selected template or settings. You do not need to type company information repeatedly.
